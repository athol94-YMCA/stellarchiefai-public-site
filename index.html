StellarChief AI, also called n8n Personal Hub in its Google authorization settings, supports its authorized owner with research, project coordination, structured decisions, and personal development.

This is a public informational website, not an assistant login. The private assistant, authentication, automation services, and connected Google accounts are hosted separately. There is no public registration or access to personal records here.

What the private system is designed to do

01 / Review

Find relevant information

With permission, review authorized email, calendar, and selected files for grounded answers and follow-ups.

02 / Organize

Track decisions and work

Maintain context for projects, milestones, priorities, and evidence-based progress across sessions.

03 / Prepare

Recommend actions

Prepare drafts and next steps, with owner approval for consequential external actions.

Connected services and privacy

The private application can use authorized Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Calendar connections within the permissions granted by each account. The public website does not provide account connections or receive Google account content.

Read the privacy policy for details about processing, storage, and connected services.

The application’s use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
